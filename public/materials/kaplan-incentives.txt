General overview (check it against Kaplan's lecture):
- Principal–agent problem: a principal (owner, employer) hires an agent whose effort is hard to observe and whose goals differ.
- Contracts align incentives: piece rates, commissions, bonuses, profit sharing, stock options.
- Trade-off: stronger pay-for-performance improves effort but loads risk onto the agent, who may need to be paid more to accept it.
- Efficiency wages: paying above market makes the job valuable to keep, which discourages shirking.
- Watch for gaming: people optimize what is measured (Kaplan: "people respond to incentives, often in unintended ways").
