Public goods and common resources · ECON 351x, Prof. Matthew Hill (Exam 3)
Study Hub topic brief. Source: Hill Types of Goods slides; Exam 3 Public Goods & Common Resources practice set.

Classify goods by two questions: is it rival (my use leaves less for you)? Is it excludable (can non-payers be kept out)?
- Private goods: rival and excludable (a sandwich).
- Club goods: non-rival and excludable (streaming service, toll road without congestion).
- Common resources: rival and non-excludable (fish in the ocean). Problem: the tragedy of the commons, overuse. Fixes: quotas, property rights, community rules.
- Public goods: non-rival and non-excludable (national defense, fireworks). Problem: free riding, so private markets undersupply them. Fixes: government provision, subsidies.
- The efficient amount of a public good is where the SUM of everyone's marginal benefits equals marginal cost.
