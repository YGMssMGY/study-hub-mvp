ECON 351x · Hill · Exam 1

Elasticity

Try it: tap a row
The gist

How much people change what they buy when the price moves. Elastic: a lot. Inelastic: barely.

Key formula

Ed = %ΔQd ÷ %ΔP Bigger than 1 (ignoring the sign) means elastic.

Where it came from

Prof. Hill's elasticity slides and the Test 1 question bank, linked straight to the page.
